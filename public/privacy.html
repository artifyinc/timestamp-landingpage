Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Artify, Inc. (“Artify,” “we,” “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, keeps and deletes information when you use the Timestamp Camera app for iOS and Android (the “App”) and the website at timestamp.camera (the “Website”; together with the App, the “Service”). Timestamp Camera lets you add date and time stamps to your photos.

The short version: the App does not ask you to create an account, and your photos are edited and saved on your own device. The App does use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, app configuration and advertising, and those services receive device identifiers and usage information as described below.

2. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The following is processed only on your device. We do not receive it, except for the iOS photo quality thumbnail described in Section 4:

  • Camera and microphone. The App uses the camera to take photos and, on iOS, to record videos. When you record a video on iOS, the App asks for microphone access so that the video can include sound. The date and time stamp is drawn into the video on your device, and the finished video, including its audio, is saved to your photo library. Videos and audio are never sent to us. If you do not allow microphone access, videos are recorded without sound. The Android App does not record video and does not use the microphone.
  • Photo library. The App reads photos you choose, including the date and time stored in the photo, so it can show that date as a stamp. Edited photos and recorded videos are saved to your photo library on your device. When saving, the App sets the capture date and does not add location (GPS) data.
  • Text and stamps. Text you type onto a photo, and the stamp styles and settings you choose, are stored on your device.
  • Screen lock (iOS). If you turn on the App passcode, it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Face ID / Touch ID checks are performed by iOS, and we never receive biometric data.
  • Usage pattern for ad timing. The App keeps a short on-device history (about 7 days) of when you take photos, used to decide when to show ads. The detailed history stays on your device. Only summary counts (for example, the number of photo sessions and photos per day, and the hour of day) are sent as analytics events, as described in Section 3.
  • Website photo tool. The free timestamp tool on the Website processes your photo in your browser. The photo is not uploaded to us.
  • Share extension and Shortcuts (iOS). You can stamp photos from another app’s share sheet (“Timestamp” share extension) or with the Shortcuts / Siri action. These run on your device. The share extension contains no analytics or advertising SDK. It uses an App Group shared with the App to read your stamp settings and to keep a record of each save event, such as the number of photos and the time. The App sends these records to Google Analytics for Firebase the next time you open it. The photos themselves are not uploaded.

When you share a photo or video, the App opens your device’s share sheet. The photo or video goes to the app or person you choose, and that recipient’s own privacy policy applies.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND WHY

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account details in the App. The information below is collected automatically by the App and by the third-party software development kits (“SDKs”) built into it.

a) Device and app identifiers

  • The Firebase installation ID and app-instance ID created by Google Firebase.
  • On iOS, the identifier for vendor (IDFV), which we set as the analytics user ID.
  • The advertising identifier: the IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking, see Section 6) or the Android Advertising ID.
  • On Android, the App Set ID.
  • On iOS, a Firebase Cloud Messaging push token.

Used for: analytics, crash reporting, app configuration, advertising and ad measurement, and fraud prevention.

b) App activity

  • Events such as app opens, screens and buttons used, photos and videos taken, saved or shared (the event only, plus details such as video length and format, not the photo or video itself), stamp and date-format choices, camera settings, onboarding steps, permission grant or deny results, and subscription screen views and outcomes.
  • The interest categories you optionally select during onboarding (for example: diet, exercise, study, parenting, work, daily life).
  • Ad events such as ad impressions and clicks.

Used for: understanding how the App is used, improving features, running experiments, and deciding which ads to show and when.

c) Diagnostics

  • Crash reports and stack traces, device model, operating system version, app version, memory and disk state at the time of a crash.
  • On Android only, performance data such as app start time and the duration of network requests (Firebase Performance Monitoring).

Used for: finding and fixing bugs and keeping the App stable.

d) Approximate location

Our analytics and advertising partners may infer your approximate location (country or city) from your IP address. The Android App does not request location permission.

The iOS App does not itself request location permission. If you have separately granted location permission to the App, iOS lets the advertising SDKs inside the App read your location to deliver personalized ads. 4. PHOTO QUALITY THUMBNAILS (iOS ONLY)

The iOS App contains a quality-monitoring feature that we can switch on or off remotely through Firebase Remote Config. The Android App does not contain this feature.

When this feature is switched on and you save a stamped photo, the App does the following:

  • It makes a very small copy of the saved photo, with the stamp, that fits within 64 × 64 pixels and is compressed as a low-quality JPEG.
  • It uploads this copy to our Google Firebase Cloud Storage bucket.
  • The file name contains the upload date, your device’s identifier for vendor (IDFV) and a count of how many photos you have saved.

The full-size photo is never uploaded. Videos, and photos stamped through the share extension or Shortcuts, are never uploaded by this feature.
